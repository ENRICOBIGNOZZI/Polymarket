# Multi-alpha 2H research contract

This lane is PAPER-only and has zero execution authority.

The 2H window is selected before alpha inspection, using only causal-data
coverage and operational-health criteria. The economic baseline is the current
all-crypto continuous-PM-L1 timing replay at 5/10/25/50/100/250 ms entry
latency and 0.5/0.75/1/1.5/2/3/4/5/7.5/10 s exit horizons, at 5 shares.

Rich features are joined only by backward available-time as-of semantics.
Censored or missing evidence remains NA. It is never converted to zero PnL.

The 2H split is 60% train, 20% validation, 20% local internal test. Results are
research screening only. Promising candidates must be frozen, moved to a
longer robustness test, and then tested on untouched future PAPER data.

Pure fast arbitrage is outside this lane and must remain independent from all
statistical-alpha feature/model code.
