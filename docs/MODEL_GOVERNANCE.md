# V7 Model Governance

Authority progresses through RESEARCH, SHADOW and PAPER in this repository. Real-money levels are unavailable without a separate explicit operator authorization.

Every refit is a challenger. Promotion is never automatic and requires causal chronological OOS, exact model SHA, cost-stressed replay, forward shadow, frozen forward PAPER, calibration, mature execution evidence and zero invariant failures.

Independent samples and minimum evidence are strategy-specific. Random time-series shuffles, repeatedly reused untouched tests and post-hoc threshold relaxation are prohibited. Negative results are retained and models without robust edge are rejected.

All strategies share account-level capital, inventory and risk. Allocation uses robust expected net dollars per capital-time subject to correlation, capacity, exposure and drawdown.
