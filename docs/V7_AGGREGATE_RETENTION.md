# Older-detail retention under the 30 GB budget

The user authorized continued collection with lossy aggregation of older detail on 2026-09-09. The managed budget is 30,000,000,000 decimal bytes across `runs`, counting physical hardlinks once. At 25 GB the retention worker attempts to return to 24 GB. This is measured reclamation, **not a hard filesystem quota on independent producer writes**. A status above the target or cap is surfaced explicitly.

Only closed schema-3 external raw and normalized tape files are eligible, in validated cutover archives or numbered live segments. Both filesystem age and latest observation age must exceed one hour. Canonical ledgers, decisions, outcomes, model/config identities, PM books, unknown formats and all other paths remain protected. Source families with captured partial or overwritten revisions are deferred; their older revisions are not silently dropped.

Normalized external events become receive-time bins separated by code, run, source, session, venue, asset handle, connection epoch and event type. Bins preserve record counts, observed sequence gaps, quality flags, native price/size statistics, spread, signed trade size and price-times-native-size. They preserve minimum, maximum, sum and first/last values with timestamps. No cross-venue unit conversion is implied. Missing exchange clocks remain counted as missing. Raw frame aggregates preserve transport counts and payload-byte totals only; they do not claim to retain message content. Corresponding normalized data has separate provenance. Initial intervals are 60 seconds, 15 minutes for files at least seven days old, and one hour after 90 days; the interval is stored explicitly. Existing aggregates are not yet automatically coarsened again.

Aggregation streams bounded records and rejects unsupported ABIs, malformed clocks, nonfinite metrics, excessive cardinality, normalized sequence regressions and incomplete sealed records. Raw ordinary/large-queue reordering is counted explicitly; forward sequence jumps are not presented as confirmed message loss. The aggregate contains the complete decoded source hash. It is compressed, reread and verified before a durable expiry receipt and per-source availability tombstones are published. Only then are the exact inspected aliases and exclusive CAS objects retired. Protected references or unknown hardlinks prevent retirement. Interrupted deletion resumes from the verified aggregate. The store retains original revision metadata, and reads of retired raw return `RAW_DETAIL_EXPIRED_AFTER_AGGREGATION`; rebuilding its index preserves those metadata without claiming raw availability.

The worker shares the evidence-store writer lock with capture and lossless compaction. It cannot reopen, truncate or rotate producer-owned active files. This implementation covers external tapes; it does not yet guarantee indefinitely bounded growth of every other producer or permanent aggregate. The status exposes `hard_quota_enforced: false`, deferred sources and actual before/after bytes. Aggregate data is descriptive research only and cannot substitute for exact fill, latency or queue replay, nor authorize model promotion.

Validation includes native-market aggregates, raw-scope disclosure, incomplete tapes, unsupported ABI, recent-observation protection, canonical paths, symlinks, open files, unknown hardlinks, shared protected objects, explicit expiry reads, index rebuild and crash recovery with aggregate corruption rejection.
