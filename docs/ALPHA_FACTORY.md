# V7 Alpha Factory

The Alpha Factory evaluates research challengers for the single V7 PAPER architecture. It does not maintain or route to alternate runtime generations.

## Inputs

- exact-head V7 CI and monitoring evidence;
- causal/OOS research artifacts;
- executable costs, fills, markout, inventory and unwind evidence;
- the canonical V7 operator directives and promotion policy.

## Output

A challenger may be marked integration-ready only when its exact-head evidence is reproducible, PAPER-only, economically positive after the applicable cost stress, compatible with the single V7 execution ledger, and incrementally useful relative to the current V7 book.

The Alpha Factory has no merge, deploy, authenticated-execution or real-order authority. Promotion remains bound to the V7 integration, validation and deployment lifecycle.
